Terraria Web

This site has no game files. It runs your own tModLoader and Terraria. The game is copied into this browser once (about 1 GB, a few minutes) and never sent anywhere.

Chrome will say it is "uploading" files: it only copies them into this browser on your device.

On a PC with Steam: pick both folders from steamapps\common.

On a Chromebook or another device: load the game pack you exported from your own PC.
