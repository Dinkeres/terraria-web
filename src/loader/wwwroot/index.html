Terraria Web

This site has no game files. It runs your own tModLoader and Terraria, installed with Steam.

Pick your two game folders. They are in Steam's steamapps\common folder (for example C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common).

Chrome will ask to "upload" the files: they are only copied into this browser on your computer (about 1 GB, a few minutes, once). Nothing is sent anywhere.
